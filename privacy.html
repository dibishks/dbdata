2. Our website (dbdata.in)

What we collect

Browsing the site collects nothing from you. We run no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics, and no cross-site tracking scripts.

If you choose to submit a form, we receive what you type into it:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, subject and message.
  • Newsletter form: your email address.

How we use it

Solely to reply to you, or to send the newsletter you asked for. We do not add contact-form submissions to a marketing list. Every newsletter includes a way to unsubscribe.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical connection data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

3. Our data platform (dashboard.dbdata.in)

What we collect

  • Account information you provide when an account is created for you, such as your name and email address.
  • Authentication data — we set a session cookie so you stay signed in. It is strictly necessary for the service to work and is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Operational records such as the jobs you run and their results, so the platform can show you your own work.

Business data in the platform

DbData compiles business directory information — for example company names, business addresses, categories and publicly listed contact details. This is business information gathered from publicly available sources, not personal profiles, and we do not build behavioural profiles of individuals from it.

If you believe information about you or your business appears in our data and you want it corrected or removed, contact us using the details in section 10 and we will act on it.

Hosting

The platform runs on servers we operate at OVHcloud, with Cloudflare in front of them, and stores data in a MongoDB database under our control.

4. Browser extensions

YouTube to Social Content

This Chrome extension reads the transcript of a YouTube video you choose and turns it into a summary and draft social media posts, which you copy and post yourself.

There are no accounts, and we store nothing on our servers. The extension has no login, no database record of you, and no analytics.

What is sent to us, and when

Only when you actively click a button in the extension, the following is sent to our server and held in memory for the few seconds it takes to answer your request:

Data Why
The YouTube video URL To identify which video you chose.
The video's public title and channel name To give the AI context for the summary and posts.
The video's transcript text The source material the summary and posts are written from.

That is the complete list. Nothing is written to disk on our servers, nothing persists after the response is returned, and transcript contents are not written to our production logs.

Where it goes next

To generate the summary and posts, the title, channel and transcript are sent to the Google Gemini API. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Terms. Nothing is sent to any social media platform — the extension cannot post on your behalf and has no access to your social accounts.

What stays on your own device

The generated summary and posts are saved in your browser's local storage so they survive closing the popup. That data never leaves your computer and is not readable by us. Clearing it is immediate: use the Clear button in the extension.

Permissions, and why each is needed

  • activeTab — lets the extension read the YouTube page in your current tab when you click the extension. This access is granted by that click, applies only to that one tab, and ends when you close the popup. It is not standing access to YouTube, and it gives no access to any other site.
  • scripting — lets the extension run its transcript reader inside that page. It reads only the caption track YouTube already provides.

The extension requests no host permissions, runs no background process, and does nothing at all on page load. Nothing happens until you open the popup and click.

What we never do

  • We do not collect your browsing history or activity on any site other than the YouTube video you explicitly act on.
  • We do not collect personal information, account details or credentials.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties, except to the service providers named above who process it to deliver the feature.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending purposes.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single stated function.

6. Cookies

Our marketing website sets no cookies for advertising or analytics. The signed-in platform sets one strictly necessary cookie to keep you logged in. Our browser extensions set no cookies at all.

7. How long we keep information

  • Extension requests: not stored. Held in memory only for the duration of the request.
  • Form submissions: kept while we are in contact with you, and removed on request.
  • Platform accounts and records: kept while the account is active, and deleted on request.

8. Security

All our services are served over HTTPS. API credentials for third-party services are held only on our servers and are never shipped inside a browser extension or exposed to your browser. Access to our systems is limited to people who need it.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you where the law requires it.
